Program 01 · Explained line by line

Estimates & insurance claims

Insurer estimates are written in abbreviations and often specify aftermarket or recycled parts without the owner noticing. We go through ours line by line, say which parts are new, aftermarket or recycled, and raise a supplement when hidden damage appears once a panel comes off.

  • No obligation

Program 02 · Measured, not eyeballed

Structural & panel repair

Modern vehicles have crumple zones designed to deform in a specific way. A structural repair that looks straight but is not measured leaves a car that will not behave as designed in a second impact, which is the part nobody sees and the part that matters most.

  • Per repair
  • Any vehicle
  • Written estimate
  • Authorisation

Program 03 · Blended into the panel

Refinishing & colour matching

Paint fades, so a brand-new mix of your original colour will not match a ten-year-old panel beside it. Matching means blending into the adjacent panels, and we will tell you when that is necessary rather than painting one door and letting you discover it in daylight.

  • Per repair
  • Any vehicle
  • Written estimate
  • Time to cure

Program 04 · Cheaper, where it works

Paintless dent removal

Where the paint is intact and the metal is not stretched, a dent can often be worked out without filler or paint — cheaper, faster, and it keeps the factory finish. We will tell you honestly when a dent is beyond it rather than attempting it and then charging for a repaint anyway.

  • Per panel
  • Undamaged paint only
  • Written estimate
  • Access behind the panel
