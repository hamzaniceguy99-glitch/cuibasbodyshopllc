How we work

Read the parts line

Insurer estimates are written in trade abbreviations, and the line specifying whether a part is new original equipment, aftermarket or recycled passes most owners by entirely. It is the line that most affects fit, finish and resale, and it is decided before anyone asks the owner.

The same applies to the choice of shop. Insurers maintain networks and will steer towards them, which is lawful to suggest and not lawful to require. An owner who knows the choice is theirs is in a different position from one who assumes it is not.

On the repair itself, the part nobody sees matters most. Modern vehicles are designed to deform in a specific way in a collision, so structural work has to be measured against specification rather than judged straight by eye. A car that looks perfect and was never measured is a problem deferred to the next impact.
