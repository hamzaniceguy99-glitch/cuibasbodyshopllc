Frequently asked

The questions we get most

My insurer says I have to use their shop. Is that right?

No. An insurer can recommend a shop, and may have arrangements with some, but the choice of where your vehicle is repaired is yours.

Will the paint match exactly?

As closely as the process allows. New paint will not match a faded ten-year-old panel, which is why we blend into adjacent panels — and we will tell you when that is needed rather than letting you discover it outside.

Are you using new parts?

The estimate says which parts are new original equipment, aftermarket or recycled. Insurers often specify the cheaper options, so it is worth reading that line.

What is a supplement?

Hidden damage found once a panel comes off. We document it and get authorisation before continuing — we do not carry on and present a larger bill afterwards.

Can I get my old parts back?

Yes, if you ask before work starts. It is your right in Florida.

Can you just hammer the dent out?

Sometimes. If the paint is intact and the metal is not stretched, paintless removal is cheaper and keeps the factory finish. We will tell you honestly when it will not work.
